Adds a complete playable React/TypeScript/PixiJS edition of the Python dungeon RPG in `web/`, preserving original Python source. A TypeScript server persists authoritative journeys in PostgreSQL JSONB (local atomic files for development); the offline Android package uses device Preferences. Render Blueprint, schema migration, environment documentation and CI are included.

The v0.7 Ashfall expansion adds gold, named villager shops with fixed randomized stock/prices, buyback quotes/refusals, wanted-item premiums and interlocking apocalypse stories. Villager encounters keep their surprises hidden until discovered through play. New spells, items, keys/chests and border secret rooms add treasure, emperor bargains and King Niblet's half-catalog shop. See [the complete request checklist](web/docs/EXPANSION-V0.7.md).

Item inspection opens automatically and includes colored rarity and authored lore. Closing leaves the item safely. Full bags offer an atomic swap/drop chooser, including the incoming item. Weapons/staves share hand limits; comparisons remove displaced gear. Level-up overlays show current stats and changes; measured combat captions stack apart. Existing compact swipe controls, combat forecasts, held gear, original class sprites, themed floors and health/mana/poison cues remain.

Validation includes 158 TypeScript mechanics/API/database tests; six unchanged Python tests and 80 parity fixtures; deterministic playthroughs of every new mechanic; saved-game recovery/restart; 165 phone layout/state combinations across existing, expansion and combat/loot/dragon browser suites; real offline browser interactions; seeded simulations; native APK build/signature/bundled-asset/public-download verification. Android 8+ download and save boundaries are documented in `web/downloads/`. Physical-device testing remains pending. Render deployment needs owner hosting access; exact remaining steps are in `web/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.

Final follow-up: stats separate base, temporary effects and gear/attack-mode adjustments. Enemy views show actual crit percentages, landed hits to defeat, and the first actor. Retaliation warnings account for guaranteed kills and miss-dependent danger. Ground piles are directly selectable; item-overlay swipes leave loot and move, and stairs remain available beneath items. The original red winged serpent dragon art renders two tiles wide. Older test-release saves are unsupported.

The v0.7.1 refinement redraws travelling villagers and hooded ghosts with crisp pixel scaling, removes advance villager-death spoilers, reduces ordinary chests to one or two per floor, collects all floor coins and gold rewards directly into the purse, adds a coin icon/count to the hero panel, and enables swipe/keyboard movement past active chest prompts. New full-bag/mixed-pile/chest-reward regressions and real touch checks across five phone sizes cover this behavior. See [the refinement checklist](web/docs/REFINEMENTS-V0.7.1.md).

Stair Keys now have an explicit name, blue staircase-bow icon, separate top-panel count, carrier label and locked-stair explanation. Standard/Rare key inspections say they cannot unlock stairs. Separate counters/consumption are tested.


The v0.7.2 market balance reduces the steep rarity price curve: a Standard Key now costs 11–16 gold instead of 28–50, and a Rare Key 23–33 instead of 78–142. Normal buyback quotes rise to 40–55% of base value while remaining below asking prices. Coin stock costs at most face value, wanted-item premiums remain, and villagers cannot stock their own wanted item. Fixed prices still survive reloads.
