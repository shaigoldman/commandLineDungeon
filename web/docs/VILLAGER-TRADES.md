## Stock

Ordinary villagers still carry 3–5 items. Ordinary slots roll 90% Uncommon and 10% Common. Half of shops replace one slot with a Rare (80%) or Ultrarare (20%) item. After each slot's rarity is chosen, there is an 80% chance to select from that villager's specialty within that exact tier. Other slots use the usual loot pool; a specialty without candidates in a tier falls back to that pool. There are no extra premium rolls. Legendary rewards and Stair Keys never enter random stock. A transformed key carrier's explicit Stair Key offer is unchanged.

Pip's basic staves are Wind staves. Hob's specialty weapons are poison variants with Iron statistics. King Niblet retains his broad royal bazaar, with treasure candidates weighted four times as heavily when selecting its assortment.

## Selling

Matching items receive 65–70% of market value (rounded down), versus the usual 40–55%. Specialty items are accepted even if an old save's randomly assigned dislike overlaps the specialty. These prices remain below the cheapest normal asking price, preventing a buy-and-resell gold loop. A specific wanted item keeps its stronger existing 1.5–3× premium and cannot also be in the shop's stock. Wanted-item requests now come from the character's specialty.

Quotes remain fixed per item and survive reloading. Existing saved stock and previously issued quotes are honored; unquoted items receive the named villager's specialty pricing immediately. Unknown custom merchants retain the general trading rules.
